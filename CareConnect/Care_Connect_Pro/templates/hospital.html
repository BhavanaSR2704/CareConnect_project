Overview

Shridevi Institute of Medical Sciences & Research Hospital (part of Sri Shridevi Charitable Trust) is a major teaching and referral hospital serving Tumakuru region. It combines clinical care with medical education and research, offering extensive inpatient and outpatient facilities.

Key Services & Facilities

Cardiac Catheterization Lab (dual flat-panel)
CT & MRI, advanced radiology
Multiple ICUs: cardiac, neonatal, neuro
30-bed Dialysis unit
24×7 Emergency & Ambulance
24×7 Blood Bank & Labs (NABL)

Academic & Accreditation

The hospital is affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) and functions as a teaching hospital. It holds recognitions and accreditations (NABH, NABL, NAAC) and plays an active role in training medical, nursing and allied health students.
